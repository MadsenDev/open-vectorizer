# Provenance

The spider-logo raster used to discover this regression was generated by ChatGPT for the project maintainer and supplied directly during development.

The maintainer has confirmed that it may be used and redistributed as a benchmark/regression asset for Open Vectorizer.

The committed raster should be treated as the original test artwork for this fixture, not as a scraped third-party logo.
