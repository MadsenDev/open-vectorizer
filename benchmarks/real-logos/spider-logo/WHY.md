# Why this case matters

Synthetic geometry currently makes Open Vectorizer look strongest where its design is already strongest. This mark is useful because a human sees a comparatively simple logo while the current engine sees difficult thin filled regions, many nearby contours, and no reusable structural relationship between mirrored parts.

Keeping an embarrassing real-world failure in the benchmark corpus is intentional. Improvements should be measured against cases the engine does not already know how to win.
